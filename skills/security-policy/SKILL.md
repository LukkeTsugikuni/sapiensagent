# Security Policy

Keep policy centralized and fail closed. Classify read, external write, destructive, secret input, and prompt-injection risks. Redact credentials and raw media from logs by default, validate paths and URLs including redirects, require approvals immediately before mutations, and test denied as well as allowed paths.
